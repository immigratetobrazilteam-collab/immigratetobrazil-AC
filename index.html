Immigration Notes

Acre attracts immigrants from neighboring Peru and Bolivia for agricultural opportunities. Recent influx includes Venezuelan refugees via border routes. Immigration cases often involve environmental conservation visas or family reunification with indigenous communities. Challenges include remote access to services and integration with local tribes. Opportunities in sustainable development projects for skilled immigrants.
